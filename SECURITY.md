# Security policy

Truthifi's MCP server is hosted at `https://api.truthifi.com/mcp`. This repository contains documentation and configuration only.

**Report a vulnerability:** email **security@truthifi.com** with steps to reproduce. Please don't open a public issue or include other people's data.

**In scope:** `https://api.truthifi.com/mcp`, its OAuth and `/.well-known/` endpoints, and truthifi.com, tested only with accounts you own.

**Out of scope:** other users' data, denial of service, automated or high-volume scanning, social engineering, and third-party services Truthifi uses (such as data aggregators).

**Safe harbor:** Truthifi will not pursue legal action for good-faith research that follows this policy.

More: <https://truthifi.com/security>.
